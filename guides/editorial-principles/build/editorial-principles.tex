\section*{How to use this guide}

\initial{E}{very edition} in the series follows these rules. The editions do
not repeat them: an edition's method section gives only what is particular
to its piece, and cites a rule by number where it matters (‘principles
10.1’ is rule 1 of section 10). Where an edition departs from a rule, its
method or critical notes say so and give the reason.

The guide is generated from the series repository, where the rules are kept
and revised. A rule changes here first, and every edition follows.
